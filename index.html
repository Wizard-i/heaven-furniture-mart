The Sovereign Standard / 02

Built for Generations,
never for a warehouse rack.

We reject the disposable culture of flat-pack particle board. Every commission is custom-dimensioned to your architectural space and guaranteed to endure.

01

Custom Spatial Geometry

We measure your ceiling height, door clearances, and natural lighting before crafting. Zero standard catalog dimensions.

02

Solid Heartwood Guarantee

Strictly zero MDF, sawdust board, or hollow fillers. 100% seasoned Chittagong and Burma Segun timber with anti-termite lifetime backing.

03

Interlocking Tenon Joinery

Hand-mortised interlocking joints that expand and contract naturally with Chattogram's seasonal humidity without loosening or creaking.

04

White-Glove Installation

Delivered and professionally leveled in your home by our senior cabinetmakers. We take full responsibility from log to living room.

The Master's Pledge / 06

"When you build with genuine seasoned teak, you are not just building furniture—you are creating a family heirloom."

When I established Heaven Furniture Mart on Agrabad Access Road over 16 years ago, I made a personal pledge to the homeowners of Chattogram: every single creation bearing our stamp would be built from pure solid timber. No sawdust board, no cheap glue, and no shortcuts.

Today, with over 350 completed luxury home commissions across Nasirabad, Khulshi, and Agrabad, that pledge stands as solid as our teak. I personally inspect every interlocking joint before our pieces enter your residence.
