THE ART OF BESPOKE

From Raw Timber to Living Heirloom in 4 Steps.

Our transparent, client-centric design journey ensures you are intimately involved in selecting wood grains, finishes, and dimensions.

STEP 01 — VISION & MATERIAL SELECTION

In-Person Consultation & Floorplan Audit

Meet our senior furniture designers at our Agrabad Access Road studio or request a home visit. We examine your floorplans, light angles, wood stain preferences, and fabric swatches.

  • Precise room measurement & layout planning
  • Inspection of premium seasoned wood samples
  • Initial budget, timeline, and custom wood stain selection
STEP 02 — DESIGN & PLANNING

Detailed Layout & Wood Selection

Our furniture designers collaborate with you on tailored layouts, proportions, and wood grain matching to ensure each piece fits your home architecture seamlessly.

  • Tailored dimensioning and proportional space planning
  • Timber grain matching and seasoning verification
  • 100% fixed transparent pricing prior to crafting
STEP 03 — ARTISAN WOODWORKING

Master Hand-Carving & Hand-Lapped Finishes

Our master woodworkers cut, shape, and hand-join your furniture using ancient mortise-and-tenon techniques. We invite you to visit our Agrabad Access Road studio during production to inspect the craftsmanship.

  • Traditional wooden joinery for enduring longevity
  • Multi-coat organic lacquer finish (matte, satin, or gloss)
  • Open-door showroom & workshop inspection policy
STEP 04 — DELIVERY & INSTALLATION

White-Glove Delivery & Setup

Our logistics team carefully transports, positions, and hand-polishes your furniture in your home across Chattogram.

  • Protective padded white-glove home transport
  • Final in-home inspection & surface buffing
  • Complete setup and care guidance provided
