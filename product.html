Woven Hanging Egg Chair

A sovereign masterwork of organic curved teak architecture and hand-threaded natural rattan webbing. Suspended from a reinforced high-tensile ceiling anchor or paired with a solid teak freestanding cradle.

Select Timber Heartwood: Chittagong Teak (Honey Amber)

Standard Dimensions 110cm (W) × 75cm (D) × 130cm (H)

Harvested exclusively from seasoned mature teak forests. Saturated in natural resins and kiln-dried to 12% equilibrium moisture content to guarantee 100% resistance against Chattogram's coastal monsoons, warping, and swelling.
Every structural angle is interlocking wood-to-wood joinery locked with oak dowels. We reject mass-production staples, chipboard dowels, or synthetic hot-melts that degrade after 2 years.
Each completed commission is issued a stamped certificate of authenticity signed directly by founder Abul Kalam Bhuiyan, pledging a full 25-year structural integrity guarantee.
