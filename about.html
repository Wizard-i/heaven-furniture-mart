The Founder's Philosophy

"When you build with real heartwood, the piece honors both the tree and the home."

Born and raised in the woodworking traditions of greater Chattogram, Abul Kalam Bhuiyan began his journey with a simple conviction: modern mass-production was stripping furniture of its soul. Particleboards, laminate stickers, and synthetic glues were deteriorating within a few years of coastal humidity.

He founded Heaven Furniture Mart on Agrabad Access Road to restore traditional joinery: solid mortise and tenon joints, kiln-dried Burma and Chittagong Teak, and hand-chiseled detailing that improves in beauty as the wood ages.

Our Unbroken Word

Four Guarantees to Every Client.

Crafting luxury furniture requires mutual trust. Here is what we commit in writing for every commission:

01

25-Year Wood Integrity Warranty

We warrant that our solid heartwood timbers will remain structurally sound, crack-free, and termite-resistant for a quarter-century.

02

Zero Particle Board or Synthetic Fillers

We never allow hollow composites or cheap sawdust boards into our atelier. Every shelf, drawer bottom, and frame is genuine solid timber.

03

Complimentary In-Home Spatial Survey

Our senior designers visit your residence in Chattogram to measure exact doorways, wall dimensions, and lighting before building.

04

Direct Access to the Founder

Abul Kalam Bhuiyan remains actively involved in every project. You can speak with him directly about timber selection or design revisions.
